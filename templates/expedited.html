How to get expedited review

  1. Submit your project via GitHub Issues and pick your tier in the form.
  2. Donate via Cash App: Cash App — {{ cashapp_url }} (opens in a new tab).
  3. Email {{ contact_email }} with your Cash App transaction code, the project name, and your GitHub issue number.
  4. Once the transaction code is verified against the donation and the issue, your submission moves into the expedited queue for its tier. The review result is posted on your GitHub issue.

Same-day tier: submit and verify before 12pm ET for same-day review; after that, review is the next day. Wait times are general estimates from verification, not from opening the issue, and are not guarantees.
